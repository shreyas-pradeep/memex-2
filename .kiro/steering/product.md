# Product Vision: Memex 2.0 (Kiroween 2026 — Resurrection Track)

## Core Concept
Memex 2.0 resurrects Vannevar Bush's unbuilt 1945 "Memex" machine for the age of Agentic AI. It is an associative visual knowledge environment where ideas live as cards on an infinite microfilm canvas and are connected into named, step-by-step **Associative Trails**.

## Target Persona & Problem
Researchers, investigative journalists, and thesis students drowning in 50+ disconnected notes/PDFs. Traditional folders hide serendipitous connections.

## The 3 Pillars
1. **Infinite Archival Canvas:** Spatial cards (Notes, Quotes, Clips, Artifacts) arranged freely with zero hierarchy.
2. **Vannevar Bush "Trails":** Named, ordered sequences of connected cards (`Card A -> Card B -> Card C`) with a cinematic **Trail Playback Mode** that glides the camera from stop to stop with narrative synthesis.
3. **The Ghost in the Machine (Autonomous AI):** A background associative engine that analyzes isolated cards, detects non-obvious conceptual bridges (contradictions, hidden themes, temporal links), and proactively draws glowing "Ghost Trails" without waiting for a chat prompt.
